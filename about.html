What is AST Novels?

AST Novels is a free online library of novels and stories in English and Urdu — from timeless classics to original kahaniyan. Everything is free to read, with no account and no sign-up. Open a book, and read.

How reading works

Every novel opens in a calm, distraction-free reader. You can switch between day, sepia, and night themes, adjust the type size, and jump between chapters. Your reading progress and bookmarks are saved privately on your own device — nothing ever leaves your browser.

Install it like an app

AST Novels is a Progressive Web App. On Android, tap Install when the banner appears (or use your browser's Add to Home screen option). On iPhone, open the Share menu in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen. Once installed, the library works offline after your first visit.

Content & copyright

Our launch collection features public-domain classics — works whose copyright has expired, such as stories by O. Henry and Edgar Allan Poe — alongside original stories written for AST Novels. If you are a rights holder and believe any content on this site infringes your rights, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

Who makes this?

AST Novels is crafted by AST Family Studio — the same studio behind AST Deen and AST PDF & QR Studio. We build quiet, useful things for readers.
