What is AST Novels?

AST Novels is a free online library of novels and stories in English and Urdu — from timeless classics to original kahaniyan. Everything is free to read, with no account and no sign-up. Open a book, and read.

How reading works

Every novel opens in a calm, distraction-free reader. You can switch between day, sepia, and night themes, adjust the type size, and jump between chapters. Your reading progress and bookmarks are saved privately on your own device — nothing ever leaves your browser.

Install it like an app

AST Novels is a Progressive Web App. On Android, tap Install when the banner appears (or use your browser's Add to Home screen option). On iPhone, open the Share menu in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen. Once installed, the library works offline after your first visit.

Content & copyright

Our launch collection features public-domain classics — works whose copyright has expired, such as stories by O. Henry and Edgar Allan Poe — alongside original stories written for AST Novels. If you are a rights holder and believe any content on this site infringes your rights, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

Our curation standard

We add books slowly and deliberately. Every title must pass three tests: lasting value (is it still read and loved decades later?), text quality (is the edition clean, complete, and correctly attributed?), and reader fit (does it serve our English and Urdu readers?). We would rather shelve fifteen great books than fifteen hundred mediocre ones.

How we handle the texts

Public-domain editions are proofread against standard printed texts before publishing. Urdu works are presented in the original script with careful attention to typography — because Ghalib deserves better than broken rendering. Where translations are involved, we credit the translator and keep the original attribution intact. If you spot a typo or a textual issue, tell us; corrections are made promptly and credited.

Why free, why private

Literature is humanity's shared inheritance. Putting classics behind accounts, paywalls, and trackers would betray what a library is for. AST Novels runs without user accounts by design: your bookmarks, progress, and preferences live only in your browser. We cannot sell, leak, or misuse data we never collect. The project is sustained as a labor of love by its creator — not by your attention.

The person behind the shelves

AST Novels was created by Arman Ishaq, a 17-year-old student and the founder of AST Family Studio, who also built AST Deen (Islamic tools) and AST PDF & QR Studio (free utilities). What began as a personal reading project — a beautiful place to read Manto and Austen on a phone — grew into a public library. Every page is hand-crafted: no templates-for-hire, no AI-generated filler content, no dark patterns.

Who makes this?

AST Novels is crafted by AST Family Studio — the same studio behind AST Deen and AST PDF & QR Studio. We build quiet, useful things for readers.
