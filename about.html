What is AST Library?

AST Library is the home of the novels of Arman Ishaq — four in Urdu, two in English. Everything is free to read, with no account and no sign-up. Open a book, and read.

How reading works

Every novel opens in a calm, distraction-free reader. You can switch between day, sepia, and night themes, adjust the type size, and jump between chapters. Your reading progress and bookmarks are saved privately on your own device — nothing ever leaves your browser.

Install it like an app

AST Library is a Progressive Web App. On Android, tap Install when the banner appears (or use your browser's Add to Home screen option). On iPhone, open the Share menu in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen. Once installed, the library works offline after your first visit.

Content & copyright

All six novels on AST Library are original works by Arman Ishaq, © Arman Ishaq — All Rights Reserved. They are free to read in the reader only; copying, downloading, or redistributing the text is not allowed.

How the books are written

Every novel here is written by one author, Arman Ishaq, who began his first novel at 15. Each book is written chapter by chapter, read many times over, and published only when it meets his standard: no filler, no shortcuts — a suspense hook at the end of every chapter.

How we handle the texts

Urdu works are presented in the original script with careful Nastaliq typography; English works are presented in clean serif type. If you spot a typo or a textual issue, tell us; corrections are made promptly.

Why free, why private

These novels are free to read because their author wants them read. AST Library runs without user accounts by design: your bookmarks, progress, and preferences live only in your browser. We cannot sell, leak, or misuse data we never collect. The project is sustained as a labor of love by its creator — not by your attention.

The person behind the shelves

Written by Arman Ishaq — a 17-year-old author from Swabi, Pakistan, who wrote his first novel at 15. Arman Ishaq is a 17-year-old student and the founder of AST Family Studio, who also built AST Deen (Islamic tools) and AST PDF & QR Studio (free utilities). What began as a teenager writing his first novel at 15 has become a library of six novels. Every page is hand-crafted: no templates-for-hire, no AI-generated filler content, no dark patterns.

Who makes this?

AST Library is crafted by AST Family Studio — the same studio behind AST Deen and AST PDF & QR Studio. We build quiet, useful things for readers.
