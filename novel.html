How to Read a Novel Free on AST Novels

Every book on AST Novels opens in a distraction-free reader built for long reading sessions — day, sepia and night themes, adjustable type size, and automatic bookmarks that remember exactly where you left off, even if you close the tab. Books you have opened keep working offline once installed.

Not sure what to read next? Browse the full library and filter by language or genre, or explore the Urdu novels collection — Manto's afsanay, Iqbal's poetry and Ghalib's ghazals in original Urdu script. English readers can pick up Sherlock Holmes, Pride and Prejudice or Dracula.

Curious about the writer behind the book? The authors page has short biographies of Jane Austen, Arthur Conan Doyle, Mary Shelley, Saadat Hasan Manto and more. Everything on AST Novels is free — no signup, no app download, no payment. Learn how the library works.
