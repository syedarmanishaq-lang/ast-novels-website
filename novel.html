How to Read a Novel Free on AST Library

Every book on AST Library opens in a distraction-free reader built for long reading sessions — day, sepia and night themes, adjustable type size, and automatic bookmarks that remember exactly where you left off, even if you close the tab. Books you have opened keep working offline once installed.

Not sure what to read next? Browse the full library and filter by language or genre, or explore the Urdu novels collection — Amanat-e-Noor, Jala Hua Khat, Har Jahan and Wo Diary in original Urdu script. English readers can pick up The Trust of Light or The Burnt Letter.

Curious about the writer behind the book? The author page tells the story of Arman Ishaq, who wrote his first novel at 15. Everything on AST Library is free — no signup, no app download, no payment. Learn how the library works.
