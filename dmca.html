DMCA & Copyright

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Our respect for rights holders

AST Novels is built on public-domain classics — works whose copyright has expired — and original stories written for this library. We respect the rights of authors and publishers, and we take copyright complaints seriously. If you are a rights holder and believe any content on this site infringes your rights, please contact us using the process below instead of taking other action. Your complaint will be honored: confirmed infringing content is removed promptly.

How to file a takedown notice

Send a notice to contact.astfamily@gmail.com with the subject line "DMCA Takedown Request". A valid notice must include all of the following:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed (title, author, and any registration details if available).
  • The exact URL(s) on our site where the allegedly infringing material appears (for example, the novel or chapter page link).
  • Your contact information: full name, email address, and mailing address.
  • A good-faith statement that you believe the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • An accuracy statement: that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Notices missing key details may take longer to process, so please include everything above.

What happens next

We review every complete notice promptly. Material confirmed to infringe is removed from the site without delay. We may also notify the person who provided the material and keep a record of the complaint.

Counter-notification

If your material was removed and you believe the removal was a mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notice to the same email address. It must include: identification of the removed material and its former URL, a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake, your name and contact information, a statement consenting to the jurisdiction of your local courts, and your physical or electronic signature. We will forward valid counter-notices to the original complainant.

Repeat infringers

Accounts or contributors who repeatedly upload infringing material will have their uploading privileges terminated.

A note of thanks

We say a special thanks to all copyright owners who allow their work to be shared freely with readers — and to those who reach out to us directly so issues can be resolved quickly and fairly.

Contact
