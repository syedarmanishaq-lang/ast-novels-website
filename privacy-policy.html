Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

The short version

AST Novels collects almost nothing about you. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no tracking profiles. Your bookmarks and reading progress are stored only in your own browser, on your own device.

What we store

  • On your device only: reading progress, bookmarks, and reader preferences (theme, font size). This data never leaves your browser and is never sent to us.
  • Anonymous analytics: we may use privacy-friendly, aggregate statistics (such as pages visited) to understand which stories readers love. This data cannot identify you.

What we never do

  • We never ask for your name, email, or any personal information.
  • We never sell data — there is nothing to sell.
  • We never show personalized ads based on your reading.

Cookies & local storage

We use your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies) solely to remember your place in a novel and your reading preferences. Clearing your browser data removes it completely.

Children

AST Novels is a general-audience library. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing special to disclose — but parents should note that some classic stories (e.g., gothic horror) deal with mature themes.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach AST Family Studio through any of our official channels listed on our websites.
