Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version

AST Library collects almost nothing about you. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no tracking profiles. No login is needed to read — just open a book and read. Your bookmarks and reading progress are stored only in your own browser, on your own device. The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID and approximate location to choose ads — see Ads below.

What we store

  • On your device only: reading progress, bookmarks, and reader preferences (theme, font size). This data never leaves your browser and is never sent to us.
  • Hosting logs: the site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, its servers may keep short-term technical logs (for example, the page requested) to keep the site running and secure. We do not use those logs to identify readers.

Ads

This site may show ads served by Google (AdSense). Google may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. The Android app shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To serve ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and approximate location, and share them with Google and its advertising partners. We do not choose ads based on what you read here, and we never sell reader data. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your Google Ads Settings, and you can reset your device's advertising ID in your Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Site administration

The only sign-in on this site is the owner's admin sign-in, used to publish and manage books. Readers never need an account, and reader data is never shared with anyone.

What we never do

  • We never ask for your name, email, or any personal information.
  • We never sell data. Advertising data (advertising ID and approximate location) is handled by Google's ad services as described above — it is not sold by us.
  • We never show personalized ads based on your reading.

Cookies & local storage

We use your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies) solely to remember your place in a novel and your reading preferences. Clearing your browser data removes it completely.

Children

AST Library is a general-audience library of novels by one author, written for teen and adult readers. We collect no name, email, or other direct personal information; the only data involved in advertising is the device advertising ID and approximate location described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email AST Family Studio at contact.astfamily@gmail.com.
