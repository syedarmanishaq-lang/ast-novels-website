What to read in the library

New to AST Novels? Start with the Urdu collection — Saadat Hasan Manto's unforgettable afsanay (Toba Tek Singh, Khol Do, Thanda Gosht), Allama Iqbal's Shikwa and Jawab-e-Shikwa, and thirty selected ghazals of Mirza Ghalib, all in original Urdu script with a clean, readable nastaliq-style setting.

Prefer English? The classics shelf holds Jane Austen's Pride and Prejudice, Mary Shelley's Frankenstein, Bram Stoker's Dracula, Lewis Carroll's Alice in Wonderland, and the complete Sherlock Holmes stories by Arthur Conan Doyle — free to read online, chapter by chapter.

Reading guide

How to Choose Your Next Book

Short on time? Start with the short stories — The Gift of the Magi or The Tell-Tale Heart take under 30 minutes and deliver the full force of great fiction. Toba Tek Singh is Manto's shortest path to understanding Partition's human cost.

Want Urdu? Use the اردو filter. Beyond Manto's three stories, the shelves hold Iqbal's selected poetry — including Shikwa and Jawab-e-Shikwa — and thirty ghazals of Ghalib in the original script.

Want a long journey? Moby Dick and Pride and Prejudice reward slow reading — a chapter a night, with the reader's night mode and automatic bookmarks keeping your place.

Reading with kids? Alice in Wonderland and Treasure Island are timeless family reads — adventure, wonder, and language that delights at any age.
