Meet the Author

Born 2009 · Swabi, Pakistan

Arman Ishaq

Written by Arman Ishaq — a 17-year-old author from Swabi, Pakistan, who wrote his first novel at 15.

ارمان اسحاق — 17 سال کی عمر میں ناول لکھنے والا

He began his first novel, Amanat-e-Noor, at 15 and finished it at 17. Since then he has written six novels — four in Urdu, two in English — and keeps writing. His books are free to read here, in the reader only.

His books: Amanat-e-Noor · Jala Hua Khat · Har Jahan · Wo Diary · The Trust of Light · The Burnt Letter
