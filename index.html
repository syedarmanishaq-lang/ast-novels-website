Why read here

Urdu novels online, free — and English classics too

AST Novels is a free online library where you can read novels online free — timeless English classics and beloved Urdu kahaniyan, presented in beautiful, distraction-free type. No accounts, no subscriptions, no noise: just open a book and read.

For Urdu readers, the shelves hold the giants of Urdu adab: read Manto's stories online — Toba Tek Singh, Khol Do, Thanda Gosht — in the original Urdu, alongside Iqbal's Shikwa and the ghazals of Ghalib. For English readers, the collection spans Austen, Dickens, and Doyle: read Sherlock Holmes online free, from A Study in Scarlet to The Hound of the Baskervilles, plus Pride and Prejudice, Frankenstein, Dracula, and more.

Every novel includes a clean chapter-by-chapter reader with day, sepia, and night themes, adjustable type size, and automatic bookmarking — on your phone or desktop, even offline once installed.
